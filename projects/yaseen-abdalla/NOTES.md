## Facts on screen (checked)
- **Valencia Half Marathon, 25 Oct 2026:** Abdalla 8th in **59:12**.
  - It's a Sudanese record, and it was his half marathon debut.
  - It is faster than the official US record, **Conner Mantz 59:17** (Houston, Jan 2025).
  - It isn't ratified as the US record because USATF requires the athlete to be eligible to represent the US at the time of the race. Abdalla is a US citizen but competes for Sudan.
- **His background:**
  - Born in the US (13 Aug 2001) to Sudanese parents.
  - Grew up in Austin (LBJ High School).
  - College: Texas, Tennessee, Arkansas.
  - Has run for Sudan since at least the 2022 Worlds.
  - He could have chosen the US or Sudan, and chose Sudan.
- **For Sudan:**
  - Paris 2024 Olympic marathon: 33rd, 2:11:41.
  - Tokyo 2025 Worlds marathon: 21st, 2:13:32.
- **African and world record:** Yomif Kejelcha 56:51, Buenos Aires, 23 Aug 2026 (ratified 9 Nov 2026).
- **African all-time half marathon list:** 20th is Kennedy Kimutai, 58:28. Abdalla's 59:12 is tied **97th**.
- **Others born in the US who run for another country:**
  - Marcell Jacobs: born El Paso, Texas; runs for Italy.
  - Briana Williams: born Coral Springs, Florida; runs for Jamaica.
  - Issam Asinga: born Atlanta, Georgia, raised in St. Louis; runs for Suriname.
  - Joseph Fahnbulleh: born Hopkins, Minnesota; runs for Liberia.

## Where the owner's wording differs
- **Issam Asinga "from Missouri":** born in Atlanta, Georgia. Missouri is where he grew up and ran high school, at Principia in St. Louis. The board says GEORGIA.
  - Also note: he's serving a doping ban until about August 2027.
- **Briana Williams "from Florida":** correct. She was born in Coral Springs, FL.
- **"His 59 is not even top 20 in Africa":** true but undersold. He's tied 97th on the African all-time list.
- **"He chose Sudan because he felt it was the easier path":** that's the owner's opinion. Abdalla says he chose Sudan to race at the Olympics, and OkayAfrica says it was to make his parents proud.
- **"Ran 59 minutes":** 59:12.
