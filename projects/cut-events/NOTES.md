## Where the owner's wording differs from the record
- **"For the 100, it's 9.95":** the LA28 standard is **9.93**. 9.95 is the Beijing 2027 Worlds standard. The counter shows 9.93, and the caption keeps what he said.
- **"Men's or women's triple jump, only five or six people have the standard":**
  - Men: 7 in 2026, close.
  - Women: 12, not 5–6.
  - The board shows 7 and 12.
- **Long jump "no Americans, only like 12 people":** correct for the men (12, best American 8.29).
- **"Have the standard":** these counts are 2026 season marks. The LA28 qualifying window hasn't opened yet, so nobody "has" the standard officially.
