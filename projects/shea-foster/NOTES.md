## Facts on screen (checked)
- **2021 Southland Indoor Championships** (Feb 2021, for SE Louisiana): Foster won the mile 4:03.57, 3000 m 8:00.79 and 5000 m 13:40.36 (TFRRS; SLU record list).
  - He was 6th in the NCAA indoor 5000 m (13:32.38), All-American.
- **Hometown:** Cypress, Texas (Houston area), Cypress Woods HS (Lamar and USParaTF bios). USADA lists his current hometown as New York City.
- **Oklahoma State:**
  - 27th at 2021 NCAA XC (All-American).
  - In summer 2022 a teammate found a vial suspected to be EPO in his Flagstaff bedroom.
  - Coach Dave Smith held him out of the whole 2022 XC season.
  - At 2022 NCAA XC, **NAU and Oklahoma State tied on 83 points, and NAU won on the tiebreaker** (LetsRun, Stride Report, NCAA).
- **Paralympics:**
  - Won the men's 1500 m T38 at the 2024 US Para Trials (3:58.74) and was named to Team USA for Paris.
  - His sample from those Trials (20 July 2024) was positive for Roxadustat.
  - He withdrew from Paris, citing "medical reasons".
- **Ban:**
  - CAS overturned an earlier arbitrator who had cleared him, and gave him **4 years from 8 Oct 2026**. USADA announced it on 30 Oct 2026.
  - In the CAS case he admitted using EPO and Roxadustat at Oklahoma State in 2021–22.
- **LetsRun:** Jonathan Gault's story came out in Nov 2026.

## Where the owner's wording differs from the record
- **"I think they still won it" (nationals):** they **lost on a tiebreaker** to NAU (83–83). The board shows NAU 1st, OSU 2nd, both on 83.
- **"Broke all three conference records":** not verified. He won all three, and the 4:03.57 mile and 8:00.79 3K are SLU school records. The board shows the three wins, not "records".
- **"Didn't get to run in Paris":** correct, though officially he withdrew citing "medical reasons". His provisional suspension (26 Jul–3 Aug 2024) had been lifted by then.
- **"Coach didn't let him run at the national meet":** he was held out of the entire 2022 season, not just nationals.
- **"Paralympics":** he competed as a T38 para athlete. USParaTF says a 2021 car crash fractured his spine.
