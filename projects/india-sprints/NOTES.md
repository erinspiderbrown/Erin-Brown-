## Facts on screen (checked)
- **Populations (UN 2025 estimates):** Botswana ≈ 2.5 M, Jamaica ≈ 2.8 M, India ≈ 1.46 B (the largest in the world), USA ≈ 347 M.
- **Botswana global golds:**
  - Amantle Montsho: 400 m world champion, 2011
  - Letsile Tebogo: Olympic 200 m champion, Paris 2024 (19.46)
  - Collen Kebinatshipi: 400 m world champion, Tokyo 2025 (43.53)
  - Men's 4x400: world champions, Tokyo 2025 (2:57.76)
- **Jamaica:**
  - Bolt's world records: 100 m 9.58, 200 m 19.19
  - Men's 4x100 world record: 36.84
  - Shelly-Ann Fraser-Pryce: five 100 m world titles
- **India: no Olympic or World Championships sprint medal.**
- **Budapest 2023, men's 4x400, heat 1:**
  - USA 2:58.47, India 2:59.05 (Asian record; it beat Japan's 2:59.51)
  - India's first Worlds final, where they finished 5th
- **India national records:**
  - 100 m: 10.09, Gurindervir Singh (May 2026)
  - 200 m: 20.32, Animesh Kujur (2025)
  - 400 m: 44.98, Vishal TK (May 2026, the first Indian under 45)
- **Main sports:** India, cricket; USA, (American) football; Botswana, football (soccer); Jamaica, track.

## Where the script differs from the record
- **"They ran like 250 something":** the time was 2:59.05. The board shows the real time.
- **"I think they broke their national record":** they broke the Asian record, which is also the national record.
- **"Some 400 runners… maybe like 44":** correct as of May 2026. Vishal TK ran 44.98, India's first sub-45.
