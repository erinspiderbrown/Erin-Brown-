## Facts on screen (checked)
- **The accident:** May 7, 2019, at UGA practice in Athens, GA, as a freshman. He fell or backed onto a javelin stuck in the ground; nobody threw it (UGA AD Greg McGarity). It caused a punctured, collapsed lung; the piece was removed in surgery; he was expected to make a full recovery.
- **Comeback:**
  - 44.61 (400 m, 2021 SEC Championships)
  - 44.50 (3rd, 2022 NCAA final, his PB)
  - 20.32 (200 m, 2021)
- **Team USA:**
  - Tokyo 2020 mixed 4×400 bronze
  - 2022 Worlds mixed 4×400 bronze (Allyson Felix's final race)
  - 2022 Worlds men's 4×400 gold
  - World Indoor men's 4×400 gold in 2025 and 2026
