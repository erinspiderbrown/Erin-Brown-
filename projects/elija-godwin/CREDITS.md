# Photo credits

## Georgia Athletics (georgiadogs.com): most of the Godwin photos
Downloaded at the owner's request from Elija Godwin's UGA roster page (the original 2000 px files). They are **UGA Athletics' copyrighted press photos, not freely licensed**: used for commentary, with a caption credit ("Photos: Georgia Athletics, Wikimedia Commons"). A rights holder could still flag the video. Files in `raw5/uga/`:
- 2019 Yellow Jacket Invite; May 2019 (crouched on the track); 2021 indoor sprints; 2021 4×400; 2021 SEC Championships (College Station); 2021 headshot (opening full-screen)
- 2022 on Georgia's "Bulldog Track"; 2022 NCAA; 2022 World Championships (USA, mixed and 4×400)
- 2023 indoor and outdoor, including with Matthew Boling
