## Facts on screen
- **Rio 2016 Olympic 1500 m:** Centrowitz won gold in 3:50.00. He was the first U.S. winner since Mel Sheppard in 1908, and it was the slowest final since 1932.
- **Olympic 1500 m champions:** 2000 Noah Ngeny (KEN), 1996 Noureddine Morceli (ALG), 1992 Fermín Cacho (ESP).
- **Matt Centrowitz Sr.:** 1976 Olympian (1500 m).

## Owner's wording vs the record
- **"Working at an office doing an office job":** the film shows him at an office desk, and his father says "now he's working a full-time job".
  - Since retiring (announced March 2024), he's also been a Tracksmith ambassador and investor, a FloTrack Diamond League commentator (2025), and the Chicago Marathon pacer.
  - So "no one cares" is the owner's opinion. The video doesn't claim he has no running work.
