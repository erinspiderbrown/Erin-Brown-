## Where the owner's wording differs from the record
- **"Ran a 10.35":** unofficial, gate-timed, solo, no wind. Coach Pierre-Jean Vazel estimates ~10.7–10.8 with a real gun start. On screen it's labelled unofficial.
- **"Otto":** it's Ato Boldon. The caption says ATO.
- **"Couldn't even beat Briana Williams in blocks … now he's rolling her":** no report of a race between them; the articles say she taught him block starts. Not shown as a result on screen, only her PB.
- **"Become a citizen overnight":** the price ($90k) is right (São Tomé; Nauru until 31 Dec), but it takes ~1–4 months with due diligence. On screen: "HOW LONG IT ACTUALLY TAKES".
- **Big one, not on screen: a bought passport probably can't get him to LA 2028.** World Athletics Eligibility Rules (C3.3A, Mar 2026), rule 4.2.3: an athlete who naturalises by "any other means" (no birth/parent/grandparent link, under 3 years' residence) needs World Athletics approval, a **3-year wait from the application**, and a "genuine, close, credible and established link" to the country. Buy in 2027 → eligible ~2030, after LA28, unless the panel grants an exceptional waiver.
  - Twist: Ghana reportedly approved Speed for Ghanaian citizenship in Jan 2026 (eNCA), citing his ties to Ghana. If a parent/grandparent was born there he could be eligible for Ghana right away, but Ghana has qualified sprinters, so no universality spot. Unconfirmed basis.
- **"Round where guys run 11.1s and 10.9s":** right for the universality entrants; the slowest in Paris ran 11.3–12.1.
- **"Gout Gout raced little kids in Australia":** his viral races were against other high-schoolers (16–17), not little kids. Kept as said, no graphic.
