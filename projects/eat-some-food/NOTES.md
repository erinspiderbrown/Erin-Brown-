## Real cases in the edit (checked)
- **Mary Cain:** joined Nike's Oregon Project at 17. In her NYT opinion video ("I Was the Fastest Girl in America, Until I Joined Nike", Nov 7, 2019) she said she went three years without a period and broke five bones (RED-S). ABC13/AP package: "I was weighed in front of teammates… told during practices that I was clearly five pounds too heavy." Nike announced an investigation; Salazar denied encouraging an unhealthy weight.
- **Allie Ostrander:** three straight NCAA steeplechase titles for Boise State (2017–19). In June 2021 she said she'd spent five weeks in a partial hospitalization program for an eating disorder (Anchorage Daily News, Alaska's News Source, June 12, 2021).
- **Marthe Katrine Myhre** (owner's photo): five-time Norwegian marathon champion. She was hospitalized for anorexia as a teenager and warned athletes against dieting in 2006 (FasterSkier). She died in December 2024 aged 39; Finnish MTV Uutiset reported complications of a long-term eating disorder (NationalWorld: "Norwegian marathon runner dies aged 39").
