2008, Beijing. This supposed to be the one. That year he became the first man ever to go under ten fifteen times in one season. Fifteen. Ain't nobody ever did that. But that May, this tall kid named Usain Bolt come take his world record. Then in the Olympic final, Bolt run nine sixty-nine and change the sport forever. Asafa? Fifth. Again. Nine ninety-five. He even said it himself: "I've got no idea why I'm always winning on the circuit but then finishing fifth at the Olympics." And like two weeks later? He run nine seventy-two in Lausanne. Fastest he ever ran in his life. After the Olympics. Again.

Now he did get a gold in the 4x1 in Beijing. Hold on to that one, 'cause it's coming back.

2009, worlds in Berlin. Bolt run nine fifty-eight. Asafa run nine eighty-four. Bronze. Again. Few days later he anchor Jamaica 4x1 to gold. See, on the relay, with his team around him, he could get it done. By himself, in the open hundred? It just never came.

2011, Daegu. Groin go out again. He pull out before the hundred even start.

2012, London Olympics. He make the final. And in the race, the groin give out. He pull up and jog across the line in eleven ninety-nine. Dead last, in his third Olympic final.

2013, it get worse. He pop positive for a banned stimulant called oxilofrine. He said it came from a supplement, said he ain't never knowingly took nothing. They give him eighteen months. CAS cut it down to six, but the damage to his name? Already done.

And remember that 4x1 gold from Beijing? 2017, they retest his teammate Nesta Carter, and he come back dirty. Whole team get disqualified. Asafa got to give that medal back. Nine years later.

But here's the thing about Asafa. He ain't never stop showing up. At thirty-two, he make another final at worlds in 2015. And Rio, 2016, at thirty-three, he lead off Jamaica's 4x1 in the Olympic final, and they win. A relay gold can't nobody take from him. But that individual gold, the one he really needed? Never came.

He retired in 2022, at forty. Ninety-seven sub-tens, the most of all time. Four world records, if you count the ties. And out of every man who ever ran nine seventy-two or faster, he the only one who ain't never won a individual gold at worlds or the Olympics. The only one.
